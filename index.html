Things you should know

Entry visa to the Republic of Moldova

Foreign citizens should verify whether a visa is required before travelling to the Republic of Moldova.

Some applicants may require an official invitation before submitting a visa application.

Visa categories, supporting documents, fees and processing periods depend on the purpose and duration of travel.

Use the verification services above to check an issued visa or an invitation record.
