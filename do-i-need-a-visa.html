Do I Need a Visa?

Foreign citizens entering the Republic of Moldova must comply with the visa regime established by national legislation. Some nationalities are required to obtain a visa before entering the country, while others may enter without a visa for short stays.

Countries Whose Nationals Require a Visa

  • Afghanistan
  • Bangladesh
  • India
  • Iran
  • Pakistan
  • Nepal
  • Nigeria
  • Sri Lanka
  • Vietnam
  • Yemen

Countries Exempt from Visa Requirement

  • United States
  • United Kingdom
  • Canada
  • Japan
  • Australia
  • Germany
  • France
  • Italy
  • Spain
  • Romania

Airport Transit Visa Requirement

Certain nationalities must obtain an airport transit visa when passing through the international transit area of airports located in the Republic of Moldova.
